MATH RESOURCES AND TEXTBOOKS

ladw - Linear Algebra Done Wrong - Sergei Treil - link
A nice middle-ground for learning LA through intuitive proofs, but an approach through matrices/determinants (like engg. students have to :/).

ladr - Linear Algebra Done Right - Sheldon Axler - link
An abstract approach to LA using linear operators on FDVS to simplify proofs. More engaging mathematically, but may not cover many applications.

diffyqs - Notes on Differential Equations - Jiří Lebl - link
Explains DEs nicely with sufficient rigour (useful for engg. students).
